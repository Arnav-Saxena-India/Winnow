Combinatorics is the study of counting.
But not counting by listing.
Instead:
- Counting using logic
- Counting when numbers are too large to enumerate
Why combinatorics is needed in Competitive Programming
In CP, you will often see:
- n up to 10^5, 10^6, or even 10^9
- Listing all possibilities is impossible
Example
- How many binary strings of length 100000 exist?
You cannot generate them.
But you can count them.
Answer:
2^100000
